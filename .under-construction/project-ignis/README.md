## What This Is

A working prototype for Ignis Studio — Eddie's fitness content agency/product brand.
This is the commercial layer on top of flameFIT: selling content services and systems
to fitness brands, trainers, and creators who want to grow through design.

## Brand Notes

Ignis = fire (Latin). Connects to flameFIT without being identical.
Positioning: fitness content architecture — not a generic agency, not a freelancer.
A builder's brand for creators and coaches who want systems, not one-offs.
