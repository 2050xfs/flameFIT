How It Works

From brief to feed-ready
in days, not weeks

A tight process built for speed without sacrificing quality. Every step is designed to reduce back-and-forth.

01 — INTAKE
Brief & Discovery

You fill out a short brand questionnaire. Goals, audience, vibe, competitors, what's working and what isn't. 10 minutes on your end.

02 — STRATEGY
Direction Setting

We map the content strategy — themes, visual direction, hook angles, and platform priorities. You get a preview before any design starts.

03 — DESIGN
Asset Production

Full production pass. Posts, stories, covers — designed with attention, contrast, and conversion psychology baked in.

04 — REVIEW
Revisions & Approval

You review, we refine. Up to 2 revision rounds on most tiers. Fast turnaround — usually 24–48 hrs per round.

05 — DELIVER
Ready to Post

Final files delivered in your preferred format — Canva, PNG, MP4 covers. Caption copy and hashtag sets included.

FAQ

Common questions

What kind of fitness brands do you work with?

Personal trainers, online coaches, gym owners, fitness influencers, supplement brands, and gym content creators — anyone serious about growing their brand through high-quality visual content.

What platforms do the templates and designs work for?

All deliverables are optimized for Instagram (feed posts, stories, reels covers), TikTok, and YouTube thumbnails. Platform-specific sizing guidance is included with every product.

Do I need design experience to use the templates?

No. Templates come as Canva files — you just swap out text, photos, and colors. A color swap guide and tutorial are included. If you can drag and type, you can use these.

How does the Blaze Monthly subscription work?

We start with a strategy call and brand brief. You supply photos or raw content assets each month; we design and deliver 30 polished posts + stories within 5–7 business days. Revisions and caption copy are included.

Can I cancel the Blaze Monthly anytime?

Yes — cancel before your next billing cycle with no penalty. There's no lock-in. That said, most clients see compounding results and prefer to stay for the consistency.

What if I want something not listed here?

Use the Custom Campaign Pack or book a Forge Consulting session to scope it out. Launch campaigns, athlete series, event coverage, YouTube channel buildouts — reach out and we'll figure it out together.

How do I get started?

Pick the product that fits your stage. One-time purchases give instant access or kick off the production process immediately. Monthly subscribers start with a strategy call booking link after checkout.
