Forge Consulting

Not templates. Not generic advice. Real strategy built around your specific brand, audience, and goals — from someone who understands both the design side and the content-as-business side of fitness.

What Each Session Covers

🔍
Full Content Audit
Before the session, I review your last 30–90 days of posts, your profile structure, bio, and analytics. I come in prepared — not fishing for information on your time.
📊
Competitor Analysis
Breakdown of 2–3 competitors or reference accounts in your niche: what's working for them, what visual and copy strategies are driving their growth, and how to position against them.
🎯
Ideal Client Avatar
Define exactly who you're talking to, what they care about, what pain they're in, and what they want to become. Content that isn't aimed at a specific person rarely converts.
🗺
Platform Growth Roadmap
Platform-specific action plan for the next 30–60 days. What to post, how often, what formats to test, what to stop doing. Based on your goals — reach, lead gen, client conversions, or brand deals.
💰
Monetization Strategy
How your content funnel leads to money. Whether that's coaching clients, product sales, brand deals, digital products, or subscriptions — we map the actual path from content to cash.
📋
Session Summary + Action Plan
Every session is recorded (your copy) and followed up with a written action summary within 24 hours — priorities listed, next steps numbered, nothing slips through the cracks.
