What Every Campaign Includes

Campaign Strategy Brief
Goals, target audience, core message, posting timeline, and success metrics — documented before any design starts. This is the blueprint everything else is built on.
Full Visual Asset Suite
Feed posts, story sequences, and Reels covers — all designed in your brand system. Sized and optimized per platform, delivered as exportable Canva files and PNG/MP4 exports.
Launch Timeline + Posting Schedule
A day-by-day calendar showing exactly what goes out, when, on which platform, and why. Takes the thinking out of execution — just post to schedule.
Caption Copy + DM / Email Templates
Captions written for each asset. DM follow-up templates for engaged viewers. Email sequence outline if applicable. All copy written in your voice — no obvious templating.
Post-Campaign Analytics Review
Two weeks after campaign end: a performance review identifying what worked, what to replicate, and what to adjust for the next campaign. Benchmarks set for reach, engagement, and conversion.
Content Repurposing Guide
How to extend campaign life across platforms and formats after the initial push. One campaign becomes 4–6 weeks of related content if executed with the right repurposing strategy.
